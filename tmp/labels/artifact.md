## Fidelity gates

- Preserve the original logo, company header, borders, gutter, and one-page/four-quarter structure.
- Repeat identical consignee text in all four quarters.
- Do not include the Sold To street or city; include only `C/O BELLAS INTERNACIONAL LLC.` after the full Ship To/forwarder address.
- Keep all text inside each quarter without clipping or overlap.
