FEDEX API VALIDATION LABEL PACKAGE
Created: July 17, 2026

PRINTING
1. Open FedEx-validation-labels-combined.pdf.
2. Print all 6 pages on US Letter paper (8.5 x 11 inches).
3. In the print dialog select Actual size or 100% scale.
4. Do not select Fit, Shrink, or Scale to page.
5. Use a laser printer when possible and confirm every barcode is sharp and unbroken.

SCANNING
1. Scan the printed pages at 300 dpi or higher.
2. Keep pages upright, uncropped, and free of shadows or folds.
3. Save as PDF without aggressive image compression.
4. Check that the smallest barcode lines remain distinct before submitting.

SUBMISSION
- FedEx-validation-package-original.zip is the untouched original package. It includes
  the six labels, four transaction HTML records, and validation manifest.
- If FedEx specifically asks for scanned print samples, send the scanned PDF instead.
- Use the FedEx certification/support channel or recipient assigned to your account.
  Do not ship parcels using these validation labels unless FedEx confirms they are valid
  production shipments.
